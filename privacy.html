Ferry Rush Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Ferry Rush has no accounts and does not collect personal data itself. From version 1.1, the free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some device data to show and measure ads.

What the game saves on your device

The game saves three small values on your own device so you can continue where you left off: your current level, which tutorial cards you have already seen, and whether sound is on or off. This data never leaves your device, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Ads (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free, Ferry Rush shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional video you can choose to watch for an extra pier spot, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show ads, prevent fraud and measure how ads perform, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location based on your IP address
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system and app version
  • How you interact with ads, such as views and clicks, and crash or performance data

In the EU, EEA, UK and other regions where it is required, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with the ⚙ button in the game. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Google's use of this data is described in Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or files
  • The game itself does not sell or share your data. The developer does not receive any personal data about you

Vibration

The game uses your phone's vibration for short haptic feedback when you tap cars. This needs no personal data.

Children

Ferry Rush is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Changes

If the way the game handles data changes, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
